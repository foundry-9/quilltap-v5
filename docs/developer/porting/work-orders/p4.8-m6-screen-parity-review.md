## Mandate

Produce **`docs/developer/porting/m6-screen-parity.md`** — the complete
v4-vs-v5 screen-parity checklist plus the v4 retirement criteria. This
lane is **READ-ONLY except for that one new document** (and the
append-only CHANGELOG/status-log): findings are recorded with evidence,
never fixed in place. The deliverable is a decision instrument — after
it, "what's left before v4 retires" must be answerable by reading one
file.

Every row carries a verdict from this closed set:

- **PARITY** — the surface exists in v5; cite the v5 route/component and
  the round that landed it (order id).
- **DIVERGENCE-DOCUMENTED** — v5 deliberately differs; cite where the
  divergence is recorded (order header / status-log / component doc). An
  UNdocumented divergence found during the walk is a finding: record it
  with both-side evidence and propose whether to fix or document.
- **MISSING** — no v5 counterpart; propose an order slug, a rough size
  (rider / lane / round), and what it depends on.
- **WON'T-PORT** — with the rationale (dead in v4, v4-internal
  workaround, or retired by a locked decision) and the evidence.

Every verdict cites evidence on BOTH sides (v4 `file:line` at `02865bdb`;
v5 `file:line` on main). No vibes-level rows.
