# Work order: W4.6b — the post-office / personified whisper writers

**Size: one Opus session with internal parallelization** (the writers are
formulaic and disjoint — one sub-agent per persona family, the d1 precedent;
the seam closures + differential regeneration serialized). Closes the
whisper-POSTING half of the seams: `BuildContextSeams`' post methods,
`ContextSummarySeams` (all five), the W4.2 Concierge announcement seams, the
W4.1d2 wardrobe announcement drain, the Lantern notification (needed by
W4.9a), and the `chats.delete` vault sweep (the LAST Phase-2 deferral).
v4 sources surveyed 2026-07-06.

Every writer is the same shape: build a persona-voiced `content` (+ often a
persona-free `opaqueContent`) and post ONE `chat_messages` row through the
ported `add_message` with an exact `systemSender`/`systemKind`/targeting
tuple. The register is the *steampunk/Wodehouse* voice — the strings must be
byte-exact, in generated `prompt_text`-style submodules where long.
