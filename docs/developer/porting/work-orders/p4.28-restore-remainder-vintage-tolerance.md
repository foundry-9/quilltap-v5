  repo (`crates/quilltap-core/src/db/conversation_annotations.rs`) and

  `physical-prompts.json`, `images/avatar.webp` — character-vault files).

  the repetition suggests the parents never landed. **Diagnosis-first.**

  actual mechanism against a fixture reproducing the shared-row shape
