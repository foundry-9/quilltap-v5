## Survey-verified starting points (fresh survey 2026-09-18 on `main` `639de1a1` and at `89fcc3c0d` — do not re-derive)

- **The race, mechanically:** a publish under coalescing arms a spawned
  flush that fires after `COALESCE_WINDOW_MS`; `drain` sleeps `+ 20` ms
  then `try_recv`s. On an idle machine the flush lands inside the margin
  every time; under `cargo test --workspace` (≈577 binaries, many parallel)
  the spawned task can be scheduled AFTER the sleep returns, so `try_recv`
  reads an empty channel and the assertion sees `[]`. The characterized
  red was exactly that shape (an expected one-hint drain reading zero).
- **Call-site census (measured):** `publish_sites.rs` 57 (`.drain().await`
  + `drain_sorted().await`), `write_apply.rs` 3 (`:632`, `:656`, `:675` —
  the last is a SILENCE leg: `assert_eq!(cap.drain().await, vec![])`),
  `lifecycle.rs` 2 (`:1196`, `:1214`). Plus the `:512` `+ 60` sibling
  sleep. **62 total** — the lane recounts and records the split between
  positive-count sites and silence legs.
- **The four corpus rows, as the review recorded them:** (i) an
  own-vault-by-NAME blob read — Abigail addressing her own vault by its
  store NAME (not the `self` token) on a blob read; the resolver's name
