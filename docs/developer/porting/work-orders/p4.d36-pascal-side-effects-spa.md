## §P — the preview/pascalMeta shape (binding with P4.D35, name-for-name)

- `pascalMeta.chipLabel: string` (present only when the definition declares
  one), positioned after `toolTitle`.
- `pascalMeta.effects: Array<{target: string; previous?: unknown; next:
  unknown; tier?: 'chat'|'project'|'group'|'general'}>` (present only when
  effects applied; `previous` absent when the store held nothing; `tier`
  absent on metadata writes), positioned after `llm`, before `invokedBy`.
- The workbench PREVIEW body (the whole run result) additionally carries
  `chipLabel?` and `effects?: Array<{index, target: {kind, path|key, raw},
  value} | {index, skipped: string}>` — the DRY structured-target shape,
  distinct from the flat applied shape above. The bench never applies.
